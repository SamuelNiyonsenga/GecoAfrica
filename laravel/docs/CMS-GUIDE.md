# GECO Africa website — how to update your site

This guide is for the GECO Africa team. It explains how to change the words, photos and
information on **gecoafrica.com** yourself, without a developer.

**Sign in:** go to **gecoafrica.com/admin**, enter your email and password.
After 5 wrong attempts the sign-in pauses for 15 minutes.

---

## The one rule: draft first, then publish

Nothing you type goes on the website until you **publish** it.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Save draft** | Keeps your changes private. Visitors still see the old version. |
| **Preview** | Opens the real website page showing your draft, in a new tab. Only you can see it. |
| **Save & publish** / **Publish** | Puts your changes live on the website. |

If you leave a page with unsaved changes, your browser warns you first.

Made a mistake after publishing? Every item keeps its last **30 versions**. Scroll to
**Version history**, choose **Restore to draft** next to an earlier version, check it with
**Preview**, then publish.

## Your dashboard (Overview)

When you sign in you see:

- **New enquiries** — messages sent through the Contact page, newest first.
- **Drafts waiting to be published** — anything saved but not yet live.
- **Recent changes** — who changed what, and when.
- **Quick actions** — shortcuts to the most common jobs.

## Enquiries (messages from the Contact page)

Every message sent through the website is saved here, even if the notification email fails.

1. Open **Enquiries** and select a message.
2. Read the details. Phone numbers and emails are clickable.
3. Set its status: **New → Contacted → Closed**, and add a private note if useful.

The red number next to **Enquiries** in the menu shows how many are still new.

## Pages — the words on each page

**Pages** lists every page of the site (Homepage, About, Services page, …). Open one to edit
its text. Fields are grouped by the section of the page they appear in, and each field has a
short note under it saying where it shows.

- Headings are usually in two parts: **Heading** (plain) and **Highlighted words** (shown in
  coloured italic).
- Lists such as homepage **Slides** or **Facts** have **Add** and **Remove** buttons.
- **Search & sharing** (at the bottom, closed by default) controls how the page appears in
  Google and when shared on WhatsApp or LinkedIn. Most of the time, leave it alone.

### Homepage sections

At the top of the Homepage editor you can **show or hide** each homepage section and change
their **order** with the ↑ ↓ arrows. Publish to apply.

## Content — services, projects, team and more

Under **Content** in the menu:

| Section | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Services** | The four service areas on the homepage and Services page |
| **Project groups** | The disciplines on the Projects page (e.g. Geotechnical investigations) |
| **Project references** | Each project in the full directory — choose its group |
| **Featured projects** | The photo cards on the homepage and Projects page |
| **Team** | People on the Team and About pages — choose *Core team* or *Supporting team* |
| **Partners** | Partner organisations and their logos |
| **Gallery** | Photos in the gallery — choose a category from the list |
| **Gallery categories** | The filter buttons on the Gallery page |
| **Blog posts** | Insights articles — choose a category and a related service |
| **Blog categories** | The filter buttons on the Insights page |
| **Activities** | Dated assignments on the Activities page |

### Adding something new

1. Open the section and select **Add …** (top right).
2. Fill in the **name or title** first. The **web address** fills itself in from it.
3. Fill in the other fields. Only fields marked **\*** are required; anything left empty is
   simply not shown.
4. **Save draft** to finish later, or **Save & publish** to put it live.

### Display order

Lower numbers appear first. Give each item its own number (10, 20, 30…) so you can slot new
items in between later.

### Hiding or removing

- **Unpublish** hides an item from the website but keeps it in the CMS.
- **Move to Trash** removes it. Open **Trash** to restore it at any time.

## Photos and documents (Media library)

**Upload:** in **Media library**, select **Choose files** (or drag files onto that button),
then **Upload files** — up to 20 at once, 50 MB each. Photos: JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF. Documents: PDF. Video: MP4, WEBM, MOV.

**Use a photo:** in any item with a photo field, select **Choose image**, then search or pick
from the library — or upload a new one right there.

**Image description:** add a short description to each photo (what it shows). It is read
aloud to blind visitors and helps Google. If an item's own description is empty, the
library's description is used.

**Tips for good-looking photos**
- Landscape photos at least **1600 pixels wide** look sharp everywhere.
- Team portraits: upright, at least **800 × 1000 pixels**, plain background.
- Logos: PNG with a transparent or white background.

Before moving a file to Trash, the library tells you how many items use it.

## Site settings

**Site settings** holds the company details used across the whole site: business name,
phone numbers, public email, address, opening hours and social media links. They appear in
the header, footer and Contact page.

**Notification email** is where Contact-page messages are emailed. Change it here — no
developer needed.

Social media icons appear in the footer only for the links you fill in.

---

## Users

Every user can edit everything. In **Users** you can add a colleague, reset a password or
deactivate someone who has left. You cannot deactivate yourself or the last active user.

Use passwords of at least 12 characters, and never share an account.

---

## Activity log and Trash

- **Activity log** — a record of every change: who, what and when.
- **Trash** — removed content and media. Select **Restore** to bring something back.
